A thoughtful first visit

Kyoto, at your own pace

A flexible three-day starting point. Group nearby areas together, leave room for breaks, and check opening details before you go.

Day 1 · Eastern Kyoto

Spend the day around Higashiyama: choose a temple or garden, wander historic lanes, then slow down for dinner nearby. Start early at popular spots and keep the route compact.

Day 2 · Arashiyama

Head west for riverside scenery and gardens. Pick a few places that interest you instead of trying to cover the whole area; use local trains and walk between nearby stops.

Day 3 · Central Kyoto

Explore central neighborhoods, a market or museum, and one final temple or garden. Keep the afternoon open for a café stop, shopping, or anything you discovered along the way.

When to go

Spring and autumn are popular and often comfortable for walking. Kyoto summers can be hot and humid, while winters are chilly. Check seasonal conditions and pack accordingly.

Official seasonal guide ↗

Getting around

Combine trains, subways, and buses based on the day’s route. Check current fares and passes for your itinerary, allow extra time at busy periods, and consider luggage delivery for hands-free sightseeing.

Official transport guide ↗

Be a considerate guest

Respect signs and photography rules, keep noise low in residential areas, and give local residents space on streets and transit. Check the city’s visitor guidance before you go.

Travel Tips

A few thoughtful habits can make every trip feel easier.

Leave breathing room

Plan one or two anchors each day, then keep time open for the unexpected.

Pack for your plans

Check the forecast and build a small mix of layers around what you will do.

Keep essentials close

Store copies of important documents separately and keep originals secure.
